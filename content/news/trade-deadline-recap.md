The deadline came and went with less action than expected, but the two deals that did happen were significant.

Wild Pitchforks flipped Corbin Carroll for Bobby Witt Jr., betting on a shortstop with a friendlier schedule down the stretch. Meanwhile, Mendoza Line sold off Framber Valdez for a pair of 2027 second-round picks, signaling they're already looking to next season.

Everyone else stood pat. With the waiver wire heating up, don't be surprised if the real action happens there instead.
